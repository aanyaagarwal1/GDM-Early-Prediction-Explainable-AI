# Reproducibility, implementation notes, and limitations

## Important notebook audit note
The supplied notebook's `clean()` function performs median/mode imputation and IQR-bound calculation/clipping on the full dataset before the train/test split. This means preprocessing statistics are informed by records later assigned to the test partition. Although scaling is fitted on the training partition and SMOTE is applied only to training data, the earlier imputation/outlier preprocessing creates a potential data-leakage pathway.

The notebook is preserved as the supplied research implementation (with credentials removed and the obsolete unvalidated Low/Moderate/High output removed to match the revised paper). The published metrics are transcribed from the paper, not regenerated by this repository build. For a rigorous reproduction, move imputation and outlier-bound estimation into a scikit-learn/imblearn Pipeline fitted independently inside each cross-validation fold and on training data only, then rerun all experiments and update the paper/results.

## Risk categories
The revised paper explicitly reports continuous model probability and SHAP contributions and does not assign Low/Moderate/High bands because the earlier 0.33/0.66 cut points were not clinically validated. The notebook copy has been aligned to that revised framing.

## Dataset label and timing
The paper notes that the public source does not fully specify the diagnostic threshold used for the released GDM label. It also does not provide collection-week timestamps for every current-pregnancy predictor. Therefore, the study does not establish performance at a specific gestational week or trimester.

## LLM evaluation
The paper's report extraction evaluation used 50 programmatically generated synthetic antenatal reports. 37 requests succeeded and 13 failed with temporary HTTP 503 service errors. The reported 100% exact-match result applies to successful requests in this standardized synthetic setup, not to real-world clinical documents.

## Clinical safety
This is a research prototype, not a diagnostic product. It must not be used to diagnose, rule out, or treat GDM. OGTT and clinician-directed care remain outside the scope of replacement by this model. External validation and clinical evaluation are prerequisites for any real-world deployment.

## Security
The original uploaded notebook contained a literal Gemini API key. The repository copy replaces it with an environment-variable lookup and clears all saved notebook outputs. **If the key was ever active, revoke/rotate it in the provider console before publishing.** Do not commit datasets, patient reports, secrets, model artifacts containing sensitive data, or `.env` files.
